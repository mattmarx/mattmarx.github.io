Matt Marx is Cornell's Vice Provost for Entrepreneurship, Innovation, and External Engagement and the Bruce F. Failing, Sr. Chair in Entrepreneurship at the Cornell SC Johnson College of Business An expert on innovation with six patents and multiple publications in computer science, he teaches executive, MBA, and undergraduate classes on Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models. Marx leads the Innovation Information Initiative (I3), curates several open datasets at relianceonscience.org, serves as Department Editor for Innovation & Entrepreneurship at Management Science, and is a Research Associate at the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER). Matt was previously an executive and software engineer at two successful startup companies. (See full bio.)
