I work on reducing barriers to the commercialization of science and technology — as a scholar, as Cornell's vice provost for innovation, and previously as an executive and engineer at two speech-recognition startups. I curate open datasets at relianceonscience.org and teach courses on entrepreneurship, AI, and large language models. (Full bio)
